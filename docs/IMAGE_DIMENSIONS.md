# JESUP image dimensions

Use these dimensions when preparing images for JESUP. Dimensions are in pixels and describe the recommended exported file, not the CSS display size.

## Global rules

- Preferred photo format: WebP or JPEG, 80–85% quality.
- Preferred logo/graphic format: SVG; otherwise transparent PNG or WebP.
- Keep normal web images below 500 KB when practical and hero images below 900 KB.
- Event gallery uploads currently accept JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF up to 10 MB.
- Keep faces, logos, and important objects inside the center 60% of cover images. JESUP crops covers responsively.
- Do not place essential text inside editorial photos. Put searchable, accessible text in the page content.

## AI image generation presets

- Wide hero: 2400 × 1350, subject on right, negative space on left.
- Cover/card master: 2000 × 1250, centered subject, center-60% safe zone.
- Gallery/editorial: 1600 × 1200, landscape 4:3.
- Portrait/flyer: 1350 × 1688, 4:5.
- Podcast/logo concept: 3000 × 3000, square.
- Social share: 1200 × 630, centered safe zone.

AI-generated images should be labeled as illustrative when a viewer could reasonably mistake them for documentation of a real CISC person, place, or event. Do not generate or imply the identity of real staff, students, farmers, or historical figures without an approved reference image and usage permission.
